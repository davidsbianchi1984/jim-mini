JIM-mini ✕ QRME. A personal health guardian for one person — and, in tandem, a city of specialists it can reach without letting go.

The pulse crosses as biometrics, so the specialist answers the reading and not a sentence about it. The person's own words cross only when they press Ask them. When concerns stack, the household's own QRME care team writes the plan back, sealed in the vault. Every crossing is a choice, every crossing is on the record, and the emergency ladder never waits on anybody.
