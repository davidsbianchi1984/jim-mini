# because `clientpaths.doorless` counts call sites. So a round that adds thirty
# bindings and five screens reports thirty doors and has built five.

# the reasons for these two lived in the guard's module docstring — true,
# careful, and one file away from the list they explained. A record whose
# justification is somewhere else reads, at the place somebody actually looks,
# as an unexplained backlog; that is the shape this audit found seven times in
# the release before this one, and it is how a hole keeps a paragraph in front
# of it that nobody can see.

ios.health — a liveness ping against the backend. Plumbing, not a capability with no door: nothing is withheld from a reader by its absence from a screen.
